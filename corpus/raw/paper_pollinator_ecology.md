# Pollinator Diversity in Agricultural Landscapes: Drivers and Conservation Strategies

Source ID: POLLIN-ECO-2020

## Drivers of Decline
*   Pollinator diversity declines by `30-50%` in monoculture vs diverse cropping.

## Predictors and Correlates
*   Floral resource availability within a `500m radius` is the strongest predictor of pollinator abundance.
*   Soil health (`SOC > 2%`) correlates with higher ground-nesting bee populations.

## Conservation Strategies
*   Pesticide-free buffer zones of `20-50m` increase pollinator survival by `40-60%`.
