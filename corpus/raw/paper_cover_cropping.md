# Cover Cropping Effects on Soil Health and Biodiversity: A Meta-Analysis

Source ID: COVER-CROP-2019

## Overview
Cover cropping has emerged as a key practice for sustainable agriculture.

## Soil Organic Carbon
*   Cover crops increase SOC by `8-15%` over `3-5 years`.

## Biodiversity Impacts
*   They increase earthworm abundance by `50-100%`.
*   They increase pollinator visits by `25-40%` when flowering covers are used.

## Erosion Control
*   Cover crops reduce soil erosion by `40-60%`.
