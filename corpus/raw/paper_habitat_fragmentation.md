# Habitat Fragmentation and Species Survival: Thresholds and Conservation Corridors

Source ID: FRAG-ECO-2018

## Impact of Fragmentation
*   Species richness declines by `20-50%` when habitat patches fall below `10 hectares`.
*   Edge effects penetrate `50-300m` into fragments.
*   Land-use intensification is the primary driver of fragmentation in agricultural landscapes.

## Conservation Strategies
*   Corridors of `50-100m` width can restore `60-80%` of species movement.
