# Intercropping Systems in Semi-Arid Regions: Yield, Soil Health, and Ecological Benefits

Source ID: INTERCROP-2021

## System Productivity
*   Intercropping increases land equivalent ratio by `20-40%`.

## Soil Health and Water
*   Cereal-legume intercropping increases soil nitrogen by `30-50 kg N/ha/year`.
*   Root diversity from intercropping increases SOC by `10-20%`.
*   Water use efficiency improves by `15-25%` in semi-arid conditions.

## Ecological Benefits
*   Pollinator activity increases by `20-35%` due to crop diversity.
