# Agroforestry as a Biodiversity Conservation Strategy in Agricultural Landscapes

Source ID: AGROFOR-BIO-2020

## Overview
Agroforestry integrates trees with crops and livestock.

## Biodiversity Benefits
*   Agroforestry increases bird species richness by `40-60%` compared to monoculture.
*   It increases arthropod diversity by `30-50%`.

## Soil and Ecosystem Linkages
*   Root diversity from tree-crop combinations increases SOC accumulation by `15-25%`.
*   Habitat connectivity improves when agroforestry corridors link forest fragments.
