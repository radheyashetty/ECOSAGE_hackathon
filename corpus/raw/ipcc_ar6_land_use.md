# IPCC AR6 WGIII Chapter 7: Land Use and Greenhouse Gases

Source ID: IPCC-AR6-LU

## Introduction
Land-use change represents a massive lever in the global carbon cycle.

## Emissions Profile
*   Land-use change is responsible for `10-14% of global GHG emissions`.

## Mitigation Potential
### Agroforestry
*   Agroforestry mitigation potential stands at `0.3-9.4 GtCO2e/yr`, making it a massive contributor.

## Biodiversity and Ecosystem Impact
*   Habitat fragmentation reduces species survival by `30-75% depending on fragment size`.
*   There are severe **Climate-biodiversity feedback loops** where degradation causes localized warming.

## Vulnerability of Semi-Arid Ecosystems
*   Semi-arid ecosystems are among the most vulnerable to land degradation.
*   Intercropping in semi-arid zones can reduce water stress by `20-30%`, offering a critical adaptation strategy.
