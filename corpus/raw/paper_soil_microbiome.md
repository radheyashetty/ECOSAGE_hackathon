# Soil Microbiome Diversity and Its Role in Ecosystem Functioning

Source ID: MICRO-SOIL-2021

## SOC and Microbial Biomass
*   SOC is the strongest predictor of microbial biomass carbon (`r² = 0.72`).
*   Each `1% increase` in SOC → `20-40% increase` in microbial biomass.

## Functional Outcomes
*   Microbial diversity drives nutrient cycling efficiency (N, P mineralization rates increase by `25-45%`).
*   Fungal:bacterial ratio shifts toward fungi under agroforestry (indicator of healthier soil).
*   Mycorrhizal networks extend plant water access by `200-400%` in drought conditions.
